# Pilot: learning-rate grid on the validation set (2026-10-11)

Rule fixed before the runs: `prereg/piloto_lr.md` (commits `c0d1693` and `8c68bee`). Seed 0, 50 fixed epochs, batch 16, validation once at the end of epoch 50, Colab T4, environment of `configs/ambiente_regimes_2026-10-10.json`. Score: `val/mIoU` on the 89 validation chips at 512 × 512.

| Regime | Learning rate | val/mIoU | Minutes | Peak GPU memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | 1e-4 | 0.8902 | 10.4 | 4.7 GB |
| R1 | **5e-4** | **0.8941** | 10.4 | 4.7 GB |
| R1 | 1e-3 | 0.8879 | 10.3 | 4.7 GB |
| R2 | 1e-4 | 0.8930 | 11.9 | 6.1 GB |
| R2 | **5e-4** | **0.9000** | 11.9 | 6.1 GB |
| R2 | 1e-3 | 0.8977 | 11.9 | 6.1 GB |
| R3 | 2e-5 | 0.8859 | 12.0 | 9.5 GB |
| R3 | **5e-5** | **0.8996** | 12.0 | 9.5 GB |
| R3 | 1e-4 | 0.8998 | 12.0 | 9.5 GB |

All nine runs finished with finite losses. Chosen by the rule:

- R1: 5e-4 (best score; the others are 0.0039 and 0.0062 lower).
- R2: 5e-4 (best score; the next is 0.0023 lower).
- R3: 5e-5. The scores of 5e-5 and 1e-4 differ by 0.0002, less than the 0.001 tolerance, so the tie rule gives the smaller learning rate. The best raw score is at the upper end of the grid; the choice is not, and the score is flat between 5e-5 and 1e-4. This is stated as a limitation.

The score is close across regimes at the chosen learning rates: 0.8941 (R1), 0.9000 (R2), 0.8996 (R3). The regimes differ little in source accuracy on the validation set.

The nine runs took 103 minutes of GPU time. The full per-run record, with the other validation metrics, is `resultados/semana3/grade_lr.json` on Google Drive.
